# PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: March 2, 2025

## Introduction

Fusion Wallet ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. Our fundamental principle is simple: we do not collect your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to data and privacy when you use our mobile applications and related products (collectively the "Services" or "Fusion Wallet").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy.

## Non-Custodial and Zero-Collection Policy

Fusion Wallet is a non-custodial wallet application designed with your privacy as the highest priority:

- **We do not collect any personal information** from our users
- We do not have access to or control over your digital assets
- We do not store your private keys, Secret Phrase, or passwords
- We cannot access, recover, or transfer your digital assets at any time
- We do not track your activities, transactions, or wallet balances
- We do not use analytics tools that collect your data
- We do not use advertising identifiers

## Local Device Storage Only

All critical wallet information is stored exclusively on your local device:
- Your wallet address and private key are generated and stored locally on your device
- Your Secret Phrase is displayed only once during wallet creation and is never transmitted to our servers
- All transaction data is processed locally and transmitted directly to the relevant blockchain networks
- We never receive, process, or store any of this information

## Information We Do NOT Collect

To be absolutely clear, we do not collect:
- Private keys or Secret Phrases
- Wallet passwords or PINs
- Digital asset information
- Transaction history
- Device information
- IP addresses or location data
- Usage statistics or analytics
- Contact information, unless you voluntarily contact our support team
- Financial information of any kind

## Blockchain Information

Please be aware that any information you submit to a blockchain (such as transaction data) will be publicly available. This information is inherent to blockchain technology and is not controlled by us. This data may be accessed by any member of the public and cannot be removed or deleted once posted to the blockchain.

## Third-Party Services

Our app may contain links to external services or use third-party blockchain nodes to broadcast transactions. These external services operate under their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any external services before use.

## Data Security

Since we do not collect your data, the security of your wallet depends primarily on your adherence to proper security practices, including:
- Securely storing your Secret Phrase and private keys
- Never sharing your wallet credentials with others
- Using secure internet connections
- Keeping your device's operating system and the Fusion Wallet app updated

## Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. Since we do not collect personal information from any users, this includes children under 18.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
